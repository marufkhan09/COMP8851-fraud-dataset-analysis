SEC-GFD FEASIBILITY DECISION

Status:
Excluded from the four-day execution roster after source-code audit.

Reason:
The official implementation constructs dense N-by-N adjacency matrices
using torch.zeros(n_nodes, n_nodes). This makes the implementation
infeasible for large benchmark graphs, particularly Elliptic and
T-Social, on the available 48 GB GPU.

Benchmark rule:
The team will not rewrite the core spectral implementation merely to
force compatibility, because that would materially change the model.

Replacement:
LEX-GNN, subject to environment, adapter and two-epoch compatibility
testing.

SEC-GFD source remains preserved as feasibility evidence.
